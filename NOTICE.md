# Notice: Apple ROMs


Apple's ROMs. The Apple II system, video and Disk II ROMs are Apple's and are
not in this repository -- `make roms` rebuilds the set from AppleWin and
apple2js and verifies every CRC against what MAME expects. Nothing above
grants any right to them.

The one exception is `341-0132-d.e12`, the //e keyboard decoder, which is
generated by `tools/mkkbdrom.py` because no dump of it is public. That
generator, and the table it produces, are ours and are covered.
