# an even byte column occupies one aux byte and one main byte per row and
# needs no shifting at all.
#
# '#' is set, anything else is clear.  Blank lines and lines starting with '#'
# in column 1 followed by a space are comments; a row of art is exactly 14
# characters.  Names become labels in the generated include.
